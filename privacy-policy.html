Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

R34 Media Hub Pro is a client-side web application. There is no backend, no account system, and no database on our side. Everything you do in the app happens in your own browser, on your own device.

1. What we collect

We do not operate servers that receive, store or process your personal data. There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels and no fingerprinting in this application. No data is ever sent to us.

2. Data stored on your device

To function, the app stores information locally in your browser using localStorage and IndexedDB (via the self-hosted localForage library). This may include:

  • Your selected media site and display preferences;
  • Your vault, likes, folders and browsing history;
  • Tag whitelists/blacklists and recommendation-engine data;
  • Your consent choice (see Cookie Policy).

This data never leaves your device except when you choose to export a backup file using the built-in Export button. You can clear all of it at any time from your browser's settings (clear site data).

3. Data sent to media providers

When you search or load images/manga, your browser sends a request directly to the public API of the provider you selected (e.g. Rule34, e621, yande.re, Konachan, MangaDex). Those providers receive your IP address and the tag/text you searched, which is required to return results. They have their own privacy policies and we have no control over how they use that data.

For sites that do not allow cross-origin requests (yande.re and Konachan), you may configure a CORS proxy of your choice. In that case the proxy receives the same information (your IP and your query) in order to relay the request.

4. Third-party content and embeds

The app embeds media files (images, audio, video) hosted by the third-party providers listed above. Loading those files may expose your IP address to the hosting provider. The app loads no third-party JavaScript, fonts, analytics or tracking embeds.

5. Rate limiting and abuse protection

To respect provider limits, the app throttles outgoing requests and applies automatic backoff on errors. This exists to protect provider APIs and your connection, not to collect data.

6. Your choices

  • Decline consent: the app still works read-only; you will be asked before storage is used.
  • Clear data: use your browser's "Clear site data" or the Vault Export/Import controls.
  • Contact: this is an open-source, non-commercial hobby project. Report issues at https://github.com/or4acle/TheGoonHub.

This policy may change as the app evolves. The date above reflects the latest version.
