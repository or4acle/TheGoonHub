Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Do we use cookies?

No. This application does not set HTTP cookies, does not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party cookie scripts.

2. Browser local storage

For the app to remember your preferences, it uses browser storage APIs — localStorage and IndexedDB — entirely on your device. This includes:

  • Your chosen media site (r34_site_id);
  • Your consent choice (r34_consent);
  • Your vault, likes, folders, tag filters and algorithm data;
  • Debug flag if you enabled it manually (r34_debug).

This storage is treated with the same care as cookies under consent rules, which is why we ask for your consent before relying on it.

3. How to manage or delete

You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing the site's storage:

  • Chrome/Edge: Settings → Privacy & security → Cookies and other site data → See all cookies and site data, find this site and clear it.
  • Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data → Manage Data.
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data.

Clearing has no effect on media providers — that data lives only on your device.

4. First-party vs third-party storage

All storage above is first-party (scope: this website only). We do not embed third-party scripts, fonts, iframes, analytics or trackers that could place their own cookies.

5. Consent

When you first open the app you will see a consent banner. Choosing Accept stores your preference and enables full functionality. Choosing Decline records your choice and hides the banner; the app will still need to store a minimal amount of data in your browser to run, but the choice is respected and visible in your stored settings. You can change your mind anytime by clearing site data as described above.
